A one-line, one-star review written by a houseplant about the human who owns it — under 280 characters, funny, with a grievance and a reason for the single star.

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib) writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`:

> One star: he waters me every 19 days, then drowns me in a litre at once like that settles the debt, and still tells guests I'm 'low maintenance'. I am a fern, not a direct debit.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. Entry for the [One Star, Written in Sap](https://make.fun) bounty on make.fun.
